Every alert gets one of three answers

Push

You're awake

A normal Telegram message with a button that opens the game on nba.com.

Wake

You're asleep, and agreed to be woken

Sent only after the earliest time you set. It repeats until you tap "I'm up" or "Skip this one", and spends one of your weekly wake-ups.

Held back

Sleep wins

Too early, or your wake-ups for the week are spent. Nothing is sent, and the reason is logged so you can see what you missed.

What triggers an alertWhen it fires
Crunch timeA game you care about is within your margin (5 points unless you change it) in the last minutes of the fourth quarter.
OvertimeA game you care about goes to overtime.
RivalryA matchup you named is within 10 at any point in the fourth, as an earlier heads-up.
Big nightA player you follow passes your points mark (35 unless you change it).
