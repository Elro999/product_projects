Make wake alerts loud enough to wake you

  1. Open the Clutch Call chat in Telegram and tap its name at the top.
  2. Open Notifications, turn on Customize, and pick a long, loud sound.
  3. Android: in the same screen, set Importance to Urgent. Then in your phone's Do Not Disturb settings, add Telegram as an allowed app.
  4. iPhone: in Settings, Focus, Sleep, add Telegram under allowed apps.
  5. Send a test wake-up below with your phone locked and check it gets through.
